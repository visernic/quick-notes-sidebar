# Privacy Policy for Quick Notes Sidebar

**Last Updated:** December 14, 2025

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how **Quick Notes Sidebar** collects, uses, and protects your information.

## 1. Data Collection & Storage
- **Local Storage:** All notes, highlighted text, and settings are stored locally on your device using the browser's `chrome.storage.local` API.
- **No External Servers:** We do not transmit, store, or sell your personal data to any external servers or third parties. Your notes stay 100% private on your machine.

## 2. Permissions Used
- **`storage`**: Required to save your notes locally.
- **`activeTab` & `scripting`**: Used only to display the sidebar on the current webpage you are viewing.
- **`contextMenus`**: Used to add the "Save to Quick Notes" option in your right-click menu.

## 3. Changes to This Policy
We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
